# Estinator Product Demo Script

> Week-by-week workflow demonstration for prospects

---

## Demo Overview

**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  
**Audience:** Chief Estimators, VP Operations, forward-thinking contractors  
**Goal:** Show that Estinator changes *when* and *how* estimators work—not just how fast

---

## Opening (1 minute)

### Hook
> "How many of you have found a major conflict the night before a bid was due? [Hands go up] How many of you have won a bid only to lose money because of an assumption you had to make in hour 47 of a 48-hour sprint? [More hands]

> Estinator isn't about speeding up that panic. It's about eliminating it entirely."

### Setup
> "Let me show you how Mike, an electrical estimator with 15 years experience, uses Estinator to bid a $2M hospital renovation differently than he used to."

---

## Act 1: Week 1-2 — Upload & Discover (3 minutes)

### Visual: Dashboard with documents uploaded

**Narrator:**
> "Monday morning, the architect releases the first drawing set. In the old world, Mike would download it, file it away, and wait for more. With Estinator, he uploads it immediately."

### Demo Steps:

1. **Upload Documents**
   - Drag 8 PDFs into Estinator
   - Show progress: "Extracting... Analyzing..."

2. **Ask First Question**
   ```
   Mike: "How many patient rooms are on the 15th floor?"
   
   Estinator: "Found 8 patient rooms:
   • Rooms 101-106: Standard (180 SF each)
   • Rooms 107-108: Isolation (200 SF each)
   
   Source: A-102 Finish Schedule, p.3"
   ```

3. **Discovery Moment**
   ```
   Estinator: "⚠️ 3 Potential Issues Detected:
   
   🔴 Door 101-A: No hardware specification
   🔴 Door 101-B: No hardware specification  
   🟡 Room 106: Listed as restroom, no plumbing fixtures shown"
   ```

### Key Line:
> "Mike found these conflicts on Day 2. He has 4 weeks to resolve them—not 4 hours."

---

## Act 2: Week 3-4 — Resolve & Refine (3 minutes)

### Visual: Chat interface with conversation history

**Narrator:**
> "It's week 3. The architect released an addendum. Mike uploads it and immediately sees what changed."

### Demo Steps:

1. **Addendum Detection**
   ```
   Estinator: "Addendum 1 Detected:
   
   Changes:
   • Window quantity: 25 → 32 (+7 units)
   • Impact on Division 8: +$12,400
   • Room 103 ceiling: ACT → GWB
   
   [View Full Comparison]"
   ```

2. **RFI Generation**
   ```
   Mike: "Generate an RFI for the missing door hardware"
   
   Estinator: "✓ RFI Generated:
   
   'Per drawing A-103, doors 101-A, 101-B, and 103-A 
   are scheduled but have no hardware set assigned. 
   Please provide hardware specifications or confirm 
   these doors are not in scope.'
   
   [Copy to Clipboard] [Email to Architect]"
   ```

3. **Response Tracking**
   ```
   Estinator: "📧 RFI Response Received:
   
   'All patient room doors use Hardware Set 4. 
   — Sarah, Project Architect'
   
   ✓ Updated 3 doors in estimate"
   ```

### Key Line:
> "Mike resolved three major issues before he even started his detailed takeoff. He's never had that luxury before."

---

## Act 3: Week 5-6 — Build & Document (3 minutes)

### Visual: Export screen with Basis of Estimate

**Narrator:**
> "It's week 5. Mike's building his estimate steadily—not frantically. Estinator is tracking everything."

### Demo Steps:

1. **Quantity Verification**
   ```
   Mike: "What's my total conduit run for the nurse station?"
   
   Estinator: "Room 103 (Nurse Station):
   • EMT 3/4": 245 LF (from reflected ceiling plan)
   • EMT 1": 120 LF
   • Note: 15 LF through fire-rated wall 
     (spec 260513 requires sealant, +$85)"
   ```

2. **Knowledge Recall**
   ```
   Mike: "What did we use for hospital receptacles at Bellevue?"
   
   Estinator: "Last 3 hospital projects:
   • Bellevue 15th Floor (2024): Hospital grade, $45/ea
   • Jacobi Dental (2024): Hospital grade, $48/ea
   • Apollo Theater (2023): Standard, $32/ea
   
   Current spec 260519 requires hospital grade."
   ```

3. **Export Documentation**
   ```
   Estinator: "📄 Export Ready:
   
   • Detailed Takeoff (Excel): 847 line items
   • Basis of Estimate (PDF): 47 pages
   • Risk Register: 5 items tracked
   • RFI Log: 12 issued, 11 resolved
   
   Confidence: 85% (±$18,000 range)"
   ```

### Key Line:
> "For the first time, Mike's estimate has full documentation. If the PM asks where a number came from, he has an answer—instantly."

---

## Act 4: The Night Before (2 minutes)

### Visual: Calendar showing "Bid Due" tomorrow

**Narrator:**
> "It's the night before the bid is due. Let's see what Mike's doing."

### Demo Steps:

**Show split screen:**
- **WITHOUT ESTINATOR:** Panic spreadsheet, coffee cups, red eyes, "I think the hardware is Set 4? Maybe?"
- **WITH ESTINATOR:** Clean desk, organized files, "Everything verified weeks ago"

### Final Chat:
```
Mike: "Are we ready to submit?"

Estinator: "✓ All set:
• 847 quantities verified
• 12 RFIs resolved
• 0 critical conflicts remaining
• Basis of estimate complete

Recommended: Submit with 5% contingency 
($127K) for existing conditions risk."
```

### Key Line:
> "Mike submits his bid at 5 PM, goes home for dinner with his family, and sleeps through the night. He's never done that before either."

- ❌ "AI does your job" → ✅ "AI does the busywork, you do the thinking"

## Objection Handling

### "We already have estimating software"
> "Estinator doesn't replace your takeoff tools—it sits on top. Think of it as a senior estimator who reads every document, never forgets anything, and works 24/7."

### "Our documents are confidential"
> "Estinator can run entirely on your infrastructure. Your documents never leave your servers unless you want them to."

### "This sounds expensive"
> "What's the cost of one bad bid? One assumption that cost you $50K? Estinator pays for itself if it prevents a single mistake."

### "My estimators won't use this"
> "We designed Estinator *with* estimators, not *for* them. Mike helped us build it. The interface feels like texting a colleague who knows everything about your project."
