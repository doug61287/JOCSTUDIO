**Headline:** Estimating That Learns Your History

**Copy for this section:**

**Section Label:** Our Approach

**Step 1 - Ingest:**
Upload your entire bid package—drawings, specs, addenda, historical bids. Estinator organizes and indexes everything, creating a searchable knowledge base specific to your company.

**Step 2 - Analyze:**
AI reads every page, cross-references specifications against drawings, identifies conflicts, extracts quantities, and surfaces relevant historical pricing from your past projects.

**Step 3 - Activate:**
Your team works from AI-generated summaries, pre-flagged conflicts, and suggested assemblies—while maintaining full control over final decisions and pricing.

**What Estinator Does:**
• Reads and summarizes specification sections
• Cross-references drawings for conflicts
• Extracts quantities from takeoff sheets
• Suggests assemblies based on your history
• Generates draft RFIs with context
• Surfaces relevant historical bid data

**What You Control:**
• Final pricing decisions
• Risk adjustments and factors
• Client relationships and negotiations
• Strategic bid/no-bid decisions
• Quality assurance and verification
• Relationship with your historical data

## 🎨 Visual Specifications

### Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary (Navy) | Deep navy | #1e3a5f |
| Secondary (Slate) | Medium slate | #475569 |
| Accent | Electric blue | #3b82f6 |
| Background | Off-white | #f8fafc |
| Text | Dark gray | #1f2937 |
| Light Text | Medium gray | #6b7280 |

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, 48-64px, tight letter-spacing
- **Body:** 16-18px, comfortable line-height (1.6)
- **Labels/Eyebrows:** 12-14px, uppercase, tracking-wide

### Spacing
- Section padding: 80-120px vertical
- Content max-width: 900-1200px
- Card padding: 24-32px
- Gap between elements: 16-24px
