## JOC Market Analysis

### What is Job Order Contracting?

JOC is an **Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ)** construction project delivery method used primarily by:
- Government agencies (federal, state, local)
- Educational institutions (K-12, higher ed)
- Healthcare facilities
- Transportation authorities
- Public housing authorities

**Market Size Indicator:** Centennial alone has completed 60,000+ job orders worth $4+ billion

### Key JOC Characteristics

| Characteristic | Implication for Software |
|---------------|-------------------------|
| **Unit Price Book (UPB)** | Pricing comes from pre-set catalog, NOT custom estimates |
| **Coefficient/Multiplier** | Contractors bid factors like 0.97 or 1.04 applied to UPB |
| **Rapid turnaround** | Multiple small projects, fast quotes needed |
| **Repetitive work** | Maintenance, renovation, repair—reusable templates |
| **Task orders** | Individual projects under master contract |
| **Government compliance** | Audit trails, documentation requirements |
| **Multi-year contracts** | 3-5 year terms typical |
| **Subcontractor procurement** | GC must solicit sub bids within UPB prices |

### What Makes JOC Contractors Different

1. **Volume over complexity** — Many small jobs vs. few large projects
2. **Speed matters** — Quick turnaround to win task orders
3. **UPB-driven pricing** — Can't just make up numbers
4. **Coefficient strategy** — Profitability depends on bidding the right multiplier
5. **Documentation heavy** — Government clients require audit trails
6. **Relationship-based** — Long-term contracts, collaborative approach
7. **Predictable scope** — Maintenance/renovation, not new construction

### JOC Contractor Pain Points

| Pain Point | Why Current Tools Fail |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **UPB lookups are tedious** | No software integrates RSMeans, Gordian, or custom UPBs |
| **Coefficient calculations manual** | No tool helps optimize bid factors |
| **Repetitive task orders** | Can't easily clone/template previous similar work |
| **Fast turnaround pressure** | General tools too slow for JOC pace |
| **Multiple small projects** | Enterprise tools are overkill |
| **Audit trail requirements** | Tools don't track changes for compliance |
| **Subcontractor coordination** | No built-in sub bid management within UPB |
| **Gordian/RS Means integration** | None of the competitors connect to these |
