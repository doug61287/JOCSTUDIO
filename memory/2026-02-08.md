## Product Architecture Decision: Standalone vs Bluebeam Plugin
**Decision:** Remain standalone product, add Bluebeam integration as connector

**Analysis:**
- Bluebeam plugin = Platform risk (they control APIs, take 15-30% revenue, can kill you)
- Standalone = Full control, modern UX, higher valuation (5-10x vs 2-3x), category leadership
- Bluebeam ecosystem is weak — no real app store or SDK for third parties

**Recommended Path:**
1. **Phase 1:** Standalone web app (current) — prove JOC-native value
2. **Phase 2:** Bluebeam connector — "Export to JOCstudio" button, import markups
3. **Phase 3:** Own entire workflow — users voluntarily switch from Bluebeam

**Rationale:** JOC opportunity ($15-20B) is big enough to warrant standalone. Bluebeam becomes a channel, not a platform. Premium pricing math ($1.4M Year 1) requires owning the customer relationship.
