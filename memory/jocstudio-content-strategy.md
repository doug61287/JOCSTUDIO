# JOCstudio Content Strategy

*Created: 2026-02-08*
*Status: Active*

---

## Executive Summary

JOCstudio will establish itself as the definitive thought leader in Job Order Contracting (JOC) estimating through a multi-channel content strategy targeting fire protection estimators as the beachhead market, then expanding to electrical, HVAC, and general contractors. This strategy prioritizes educational content that solves real problems, drives organic traffic, and converts visitors into paying customers.

---

## Goal

Establish JOCstudio as the thought leader in JOC estimating through educational content that drives organic traffic and converts visitors to customers.

**Success Metrics (6-month targets):**
- 10,000 monthly organic visitors
- 500 YouTube subscribers
- 2,000 email list subscribers
- 15% trial-to-paid conversion rate
- 50% of trials originating from content

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Fire Protection Estimators (Beachhead Market)
- Work primarily with Division 21 (Fire Suppression)
- Often use Excel spreadsheets for JOC proposals
- NYC HHC is largest contract holder
- Pain: Manual UPB lookups, coefficient calculations, formatting proposals

### Secondary: Electrical & HVAC Estimators
- Division 26 (Electrical) and Division 23 (HVAC)
- Similar workflow challenges
- Often work alongside fire protection on same contracts

### Tertiary: General Contractors & Facilities Managers
- Process high volume of JOC proposals (50+/month)
- Need team collaboration features
- Care about speed and accuracy at scale

## Content Formats & Channels

### Blog (SEO Foundation)
**Frequency:** 2 posts/week
**Platform:** jocstudio.com/blog
**Goals:** Organic traffic, keyword rankings, backlinks

**Standards:**
- 1,500-2,500 words for pillar content
- 800-1,200 words for supporting content
- Include at least 3 internal links per post
- Custom graphics/screenshots for each post
- Meta descriptions optimized for CTR
- Schema markup for rich snippets

---

### YouTube (Visual Learning + Long-term SEO)
**Frequency:** 1 video/week
**Channel:** JOCstudio
**Goals:** Subscribers, watch time, demo requests

**Video Types:**
1. **Tutorial Videos** (5-15 min) — How to use JOCstudio features
2. **Educational Videos** (8-20 min) — JOC concepts explained
3. **Quick Tips** (2-5 min) — Single feature or trick

- Custom thumbnail (consistent branding)
- Chapters/timestamps in description

- Closed captions (auto + edited)
- Cards linking to related videos

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### Voice Attributes
- **Expert but approachable** — We know JOC deeply, but don't condescend
- **Practical over theoretical** — Every piece should be actionable
- **Data-driven** — Back claims with numbers when possible
- **Construction-native** — Use industry terminology correctly

### Writing Style
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bulleted lists for scannability
- Active voice preferred
- Avoid jargon when simpler words work
- Address reader directly ("you" not "one")

### Formatting Standards
- H2 headers every 200-300 words
- Bold key terms on first use
- Include images/screenshots every 300-400 words
- Summary box at top of long articles
- Clear CTA at end of every piece

### What We DON'T Do
- Talk down to experienced estimators
- Use corporate buzzwords
- Make claims we can't back up
- Ignore questions or feedback
- Pretend JOC is simple (it's complex, and that's okay)
