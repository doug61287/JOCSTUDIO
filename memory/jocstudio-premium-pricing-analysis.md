### Critical Question: Does Higher Price Kill Conversion?

**Modeling 3 scenarios:**
- Base: 10% conversion (unchanged)
- Conservative: 8% conversion (-20%)
- Pessimistic: 5% conversion (-50%)

### Month 12 MRR by Conversion Rate

| Conversion Rate | Current MRR | Premium MRR | Prem vs Curr |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| 10% (base) | $111,965 | $326,126 | +191% |
| 8% | $89,572 | $260,901 | +191% |
| 5% | $55,983 | $163,063 | +191% |

**Even at 5% conversion, premium generates $163K MRR vs current's $112K at 10% conversion.**

### Break-Even Conversion Rate Analysis

At what conversion rate does premium pricing equal current pricing revenue?

```
Current MRR (10% conv) = $111,965
Premium MRR = $326,126 × (X/10%)

Solve for X:
$326,126 × (X/10%) = $111,965
X = 3.43%
```

**Premium pricing matches current pricing revenue at only 3.43% conversion rate.**

This means:
- If conversion drops from 10% to 7%, premium still wins
- If conversion drops from 10% to 5%, premium wins by 46%
- Only if conversion drops below 3.43% does current pricing win

## 10. Recommendation

### 🏆 PREMIUM PRICING IS THE OPTIMAL STRATEGY

**Reasoning:**

1. **Math is overwhelmingly in favor**
   - Even with 50% conversion drop, premium wins
   - Break-even conversion is only 3.43%
   - 3x revenue in Year 1

2. **Market positioning is right**
   - $100/month is 20% below Kreo
   - Clear value proposition (9x ROI)
   - Premium price signals premium product

3. **Unit economics are excellent**
   - LTV:CAC of 10.5:1 (Pro) is exceptional
   - 1.4 month payback vs 4.7 months
   - Can afford to invest in growth

4. **Fewer customers = less support**
   - 96 customers for $50K MRR vs 281
   - Higher-value customers = better relationships
   - Less churn risk overall

### Implementation Strategy

**Phase 1: Launch at Premium (Day 1)**
- Pro: $100/month ($1,000/year)
- Team: $270/seat/month ($2,500/seat/year)
- Enterprise: $500/seat/month

**Phase 2: Monitor Conversion (Month 1-3)**
- Track free→paid conversion weekly
- Red flag if < 5% conversion
- Prepare backup: 14-day trial with credit card

**Phase 3: Optimize (Month 4-6)**
- If conversion < 5%: Add longer trial, not price drop
- If conversion > 8%: Consider price increase test
- Focus on value demonstration, not discounting

### What NOT to Do

❌ Don't grandfather early users at low prices (builds expectation)
❌ Don't offer discounts to close deals (devalues product)
❌ Don't drop prices if conversion is low (improve product/marketing instead)
❌ Don't compete on price with Bluebeam (different value prop)
